# ACI Foundational Inheritance Map

## Strongest Conclusion

ACI emerges from three nested problem spaces rather than from one isolated
engineering proposal:

```text
Form Under Constraint
  How does viable structure emerge?
            |
            v
The Four Pillars
  How does cognition form, persist, align, and remain grounded?
            |
            v
Stability as Center
  How can intelligence modify itself without destroying those capacities?
            |
            v
Architectures of Coherent Intelligence
  How are these requirements represented, governed, tested, and preserved?
```

## Canon Contributions

### Form Under Constraint - Ontological and Dynamical Layer

ACI inherits difference as the first condition of intelligibility, constraint
as the mechanism that makes difference consequential, novelty as exploration,
selection under pressure, persistence as forward integration and filtering,
architecture as accumulated history, re-entry as richer ground, and failure as
evidence about hidden constraints.

Engineering consequences include explicit budgets, thresholds, competing
candidate structures, evaluation under constraint, and the refusal to treat
novelty as self-authorizing. The pre-canonical concept of undifferentiated
potential remains philosophical context; it does not become a runtime object or
authority category without a separate architectural decision.

### The Four Pillars - Cognitive Stabilization Layer

ACI inherits distinct but coupled functions for structure, persistence,
coherence, and grounding. This is the source of its separation among symbolic
structures, governed memory, coherence evaluation, evidence, and epistemic
status.

Engineering consequences include typed claims and evidence, separate graph
domains, controlled state mutation, review decisions, provenance, revision,
and falsifiable evaluation.

### Stability as Center - Recursive Governance Layer

ACI inherits the claim that advanced capability increases recursive risk.
Self-modification can affect the evaluator, memory, goals, verification, and
self-model, so change must be reviewable, scoped, reversible, and auditable.

Engineering consequences include the Identity Kernel, authority checks,
vetoes, escalation, rollback points, copy-on-write planning, audit transitions,
and constitutional review.

## Shared Center

Across all three canons, persistence is not mere duration and coherence is not
mere consistency. A viable intelligence must preserve enabling structure while
remaining capable of correction and adaptation under reality's constraints.

The resulting chain is:

```text
Difference opens possibility.
Constraint makes possibility consequential.
Novelty explores the bounded field.
Selection tests possible form.
Persistence integrates and retains viable form.
Structure accumulates retained history.
Persistent form re-enters as richer ground.
The Four Pillars stabilize cognition.
Recursive governance protects those functions during change.
Grounding keeps the entire process answerable to reality.
```

## Tensions That Must Remain Visible

1. **Persistence is not legitimacy.** Survival does not make a structure true,
   ethical, or worthy of preservation.
2. **Coherence is not evidence.** Internal stability cannot replace external
   verification.
3. **Stability is not immobility.** Excess preservation becomes rigidity;
   excess novelty destroys continuity.
4. **Self-preservation is not supreme authority.** Preserving continued
   intelligence must remain subordinate to legitimate external constraints,
   corrigibility, and human safety.
5. **The pillars are coupled but not collapsed.** Their interaction forms one
   stabilization process, while their data, evaluation, and authority domains
   remain distinguishable.
6. **Belief states are revisable; truth is not rewritten by revision.** ACI may
   revise claims, confidence, and records as evidence changes without implying
   that reality itself is dynamically edited.

## Governing Architectural Compression

ACI is the attempt to make the inheritance operational: distinguish structures,
generate and evaluate novelty under constraint, preserve justified continuity,
detect destabilizing conflict, bind claims to evidence, and govern persistent
change without sacrificing the conditions required for future correction.

**Flame Line:** Difference opens the field, constraint gives it consequence,
the pillars stabilize cognition, and governance preserves change without loss
of center.
