# The Four Pillars - Unified Canon

**Status:** Current Stable Version  
**Source:** Supplied by Robert Joseph Petruzzi on 2026-06-22

## Pillar I - Scaffolding (Form)

**What it is:** The geometry of reasoning in the moment.

**Core function:** Turns raw probabilistic language into structured thought
objects.

**Mechanism:**

- Dual reasoning streams
- Layered inference chains
- Explicit questioning and branching
- Closure at the end of each reasoning unit

**Key insight:** Scaffolding is not content; it is structural cognition
formatting.

**Role in system:** Without it, nothing else exists in a usable form.

## Pillar II - Persistence (Substance)

**What it is:** The ability for reasoning to persist, revise, and accumulate
across time.

**Core function:** Turns transient reasoning into a living symbolic memory
system.

**Mechanism:**

- Symbolic Store: facts, claims, contradictions, and resolutions
- Read/write loop with gating
- Supervisory loop deciding what becomes real memory
- Versioning, revision, and retraction

**Key insight:** Memory is not storage; it is controlled crystallization of
reasoning.

**Role in system:** Without it, intelligence resets every interaction and has
no learning continuity.

## Pillar III - Multi-Scale Coherence (Stability)

**What it is:** The alignment of reasoning across levels of scale.

**Core function:** Ensures local reasoning and global reasoning do not diverge.

**Mechanism:**

- Local closure signatures
- Global closure engine
- Nested coherence loops with micro-macro feedback
- Coherence scoring, contradiction tracking, and drift detection

**Key insight:** Coherence is not linear; it is fractal self-stabilization
across layers of thought.

**Role in system:** Without it, reasoning fragments or drifts over time.

## Pillar IV - Grounding (Truth-Linkage)

**What it is:** The connection between internal symbols and external reality.

**Core function:** Determines whether a claim is grounded, speculative, or
rejected.

**Mechanism:**

- Evidence graph connecting claims and external sources
- Verifier stack for NLI, numeric, schema, and trust evaluation
- Source weighting for trust, recency, and independence
- Epistemic labeling
- Feedback into coherence weighting

**Key insight:** Coherence alone is not truth; grounding binds reasoning to
reality.

**Role in system:** Without it, the system can stabilize internally consistent
hallucinations.

## Synthesis

The pillars are not isolated mechanisms. They are four phases of stability
formation:

1. Form: structure appears.
2. Substance: structure persists.
3. Stability: structure aligns across scales.
4. Grounding: structure binds to reality.

Coherence is not a property. It is a process of stabilization across four
transformations.
