## Transform Classes

- `BYTE_EXACT_COPY`: public bytes equal authenticated source bytes.
- `DETERMINISTIC_LINE_EXTRACT`: exact frozen line ranges from an authenticated parent source.
- `CURATED_SOURCE_PROJECTION`: selected accepted archive members with member-level bindings.
- `PATH_NEUTRAL_PUBLIC_PROJECTION`: research evidence with only private machine-path fields replaced by role tokens.
- `GENERATED_PUBLIC_SYNTHESIS`: new navigation, status, attribution, licensing, or provenance prose grounded in frozen evidence.
- `CATALOG_METADATA_ONLY`: public metadata without publication of the private source body.

The active engineering workbench and the full forensic developmental-artifact museum remain private. This public reference architecture is a communicative projection, not a mirror of either private repository.
