# Phase 11 Stage 1 Summary — Integration Topology and Compatibility Scaffold

## What Stage 1 Accomplished

Phase 11 Stage 1 created `aci_phase11` as a parallel, removable package beside
accepted Minimal ACI v0.1. The package carries the provisional identity
`0.2.0-draft` and contains explicit scaffold policy rather than cognitive
behavior. Its declaration inventories name all 18 Phase 11 modules and 9
algorithms while marking every one declaration-only, unimplemented,
unaccepted, and non-callable.

## Why the Boundary Matters

The central result is separation without identity transfer. Accepted v0.1
remains independently executable and unchanged. Top-level Phase 11 imports do
not load accepted `aci`; accepted `aci` does not load Phase 11. The explicit
compatibility subtree is the only permitted crossing point. A final
micro-repair strengthened that crossing: matching class names, member names,
and values are insufficient. Source ingress requires the exact accepted enum
class and exact member identity.

## Files and Responsibilities

The Stage 1 package provides immutable package identity, ownership
declarations, four placement classes, typed errors, compatibility policy,
closed wire mappings, and differential vocabulary. Its mechanically derived
type-policy manifest preserves 978 source rows, 143 target placements, 84
accepted types, and 80 future adapter declarations with no ambiguous or
unresolved placement. The static mapping covers 26 enum families and 181
members. The differential scaffold permits only `UNCHANGED`,
`AUTHORIZED_EXTENSION`, `INTENTIONAL_REJECTION`, or `REGRESSION` and fails
closed on incomplete classification.

## Verification

The final focused mapping suite passed 11 tests, the complete Stage 1 suite
passed 50 tests, and the unchanged accepted-v0.1 suite passed 443 tests. All
four accepted examples completed. The accepted v0.1 archive and prior root
checksum verified. These are bounded conformance results from project tests,
not evidence of external adequacy or general intelligence.

## Deliberate Exclusions

Stage 1 implements zero cognitive modules, zero algorithms, and zero runtime
cognitive callables. It creates no aggregate converter, reviewer, registry,
planner, graph container, rollback engine, persistence layer, model bridge, or
cycle behavior. Five serialization-evidence-only placements are manifest-only
later-phase exclusions; their eventual mature types are not classified by
this stage. Phase 11 is not complete, accepted v0.1 is not replaced, and
Phases 12 through 15 remain unauthorized.

## What Stage 2 May Now Define

Stage 2 may draft and adjudicate `ACI-P11-STAGE-02-CONTRACT-001 — Core Symbolic
and Shared Result Contracts`. It receives no implementation authority until
that contract is separately governed and approved.

## Flame Line

> The first Phase 11 boundary distinguishes not only equal values from equal
> types, but resemblance from identity—the exact distinction required before
> a new architecture may safely stand beside the accepted one.
