# Phase 11 Stage 3 Summary — State Foundations and Graph Primitives

## What Stage 3 Accomplished

Stage 3 gives Phase 11 a complete independent primitive layer. Twenty-six
closed value families contain 158 members; twenty-two durable record types
cover identity, invariants, budgets, thresholds, relations, five graph
domains, governance vocabulary, and review requirements. Six candidate
origins and nine algorithm read contracts remain descriptive and
non-executable.

## Why Primitive State Must Precede Aggregate State

Aggregate state is safe to define only after each component has its own exact
identity, validation, serialization, reference, and authority boundaries.
Stage 3 therefore proves the parts without claiming the whole. It contains no
`ArchitectureState`, `GovernanceState`, or `GraphSet`.

## Identity Without Functional-Identity Claims

`IdentityKernel` binds invariant references and same-event verification,
coherence, lineage, and boundary-condition records. It is not functional
identity, cross-run continuity, consciousness, personhood, persistence, or
legitimacy. Identity impacts, comparisons, and non-compensable failures remain
separate rather than being compressed into one score.

## Visible Budgets and Thresholds

Five budgets and eleven named directional thresholds are explicit immutable
state. Reservation, cost, restoration, check, and adjustment records remain
non-applying. A threshold result grants no epistemic status, scale, or
authority, and an adjustment performs no learning or registry mutation.

## Five Graph Domains

Memory, evidence, coherence, scale, and authority remain separate immutable
snapshots. `GraphRelation` transfers no grounding, persistence, truth, scale,
authority, or legitimacy merely because a typed relation exists.

## Governance Vocabulary Without Governance Power

Veto, protected-change, authority-constraint, precedent, and escalation-policy
records are complete but non-applying. They do not create `GovernanceState`,
execute review, grant approval, apply amendments, or mutate authority.

## Candidate Origins and Review Truth

Candidate origin must agree with typed material. Direct architectural origin
requires architectural material and IPA-SRA-AEA review; direct protected
origin requires protected material and CGA. `ProtectedChange` and
`AuthorityConstraint` now state exact reviewer burdens. Those obligations
grant no authority and invoke no reviewer.

## Exact Primitive Identity

Every durable primitive must survive the unchanged exact Stage 3 serializer
during construction. Subclass, same-name, module-prefix, and arbitrary
dataclass impersonators fail before resolver admission. All twenty-two exact
record types serialize and fingerprint deterministically.

## Deliberate Exclusions

Stage 3 adds no aggregate state, registry, reviewer execution, audit lifecycle,
planning, application, rollback, transaction, persistent storage, retrieval,
continuity, functional identity, model integration, ARC capability, or
external validation. Self-authored tests prove bounded conformance, not
external adequacy or final v0.2.0 acceptance.

## What Stage 4 May Now Define

Stage 4 may now define the contract for aggregate ArchitectureState,
GovernanceState, GraphSet, cross-domain relations, clone isolation,
baseline equivalence, fingerprints, and comparison behavior. It may not
implement those surfaces until its own contract is drafted, reviewed,
approved, archived, and sealed.

## Flame Line

> Stage 3 is technically complete because origin now carries its rightful
> review burden, governance records tell the truth about who must judge them,
> and no primitive may enter durable form unless every nested identity
> survives the exact serializer that defines what the architecture can
> recognize.
